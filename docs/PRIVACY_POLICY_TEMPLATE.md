## What MAX does

MAX lets nearby people who each have MAX open discover one another, connect, and exchange live audio while a person holds the Talk control. MAX does not provide Internet calling, cloud relay, accounts, message history, recording, or subscriptions in the version described by this policy.

## Information MAX processes

### Microphone audio

MAX asks for microphone permission so it can capture live audio only while you hold Talk. The app transmits that live audio directly to the nearby MAX device or devices you have connected with. MAX does not record the audio to disk, upload it to a developer-operated server, or retain it after transmission.

### Nearby-device information

MAX uses Apple’s nearby networking technologies to find and connect with nearby MAX devices while the app is open. Other nearby MAX users may see the device display name that your iPhone provides during discovery or connection. The current app version does not send that information to a developer-operated server or store it in the app.

### Information we do not collect in this version

MAX does not create user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics, location tracking, cloud storage, message history, or developer-operated servers. We do not sell personal information.

## Permissions

MAX requests Local Network permission to discover and connect to nearby MAX devices, and Microphone permission to send live audio when you use Talk. You can change either permission in iOS Settings. Denying a permission can prevent the related feature from working.
